Purr & Pantry is a fictional neighborhood shop built around two universal cat truths: mealtime matters, and the best toy is always the one that just appeared. We bring together cheerful food-and-treat ideas and playthings for curious paws.

- **Good bowls:** everyday meal ideas and little rewards for treat time.
- **Good play:** toys for batting, pouncing, chasing, and supervised mischief.
- **Good naps:** cozy comforts for the important business of relaxing.

Our shop links and product listings are placeholders for now. Until then, consider this a happy little corner of the internet for cats and the people who adore them.
