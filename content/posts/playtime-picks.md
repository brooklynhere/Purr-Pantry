A feather on a string, a crinkle in the corner, a ball that disappears beneath the sofa: excellent play can be wonderfully simple. Rotate a few toys to keep familiar favorites feeling new, and make time for a little chase-and-catch every day.

Choose toys that suit your cat, check them for wear, and supervise play with strings or small parts. Our toy shelf is imaginary for now, but the fun is easy to picture.
